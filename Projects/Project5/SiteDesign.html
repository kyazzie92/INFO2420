Site Color Palette – Native Cooking of Utah

Target audience: people interested in traditional and regional Utah cooking, home cooks, food enthusiasts. Warm cream background with deep red accents evokes home cooking, heritage, and appetite appeal while remaining highly readable.

RoleHexSwatchUsage
Page background#f8f5f0body background-color
Body text#333333body color
Headings (h1, h2)#8b0000accent / brand color
Sub-headings (h3)#5c4033secondary accent
Link (a:link)#0066ccunvisited links
Visited link#551a8ba:visited
Hover link#cc0000a:hover (+ light highlight)

All foreground/background combinations were checked for sufficient contrast. Relative font sizes start at 100% so users can scale text.
